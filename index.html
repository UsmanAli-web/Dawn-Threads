About the Shop

Built by People Who Actually Run the Machines

Dawn Threads started in a small shop with one embroidery machine and a stack of rush orders. Years later, the goal hasn't changed: turn logos and rough sketches into patches and kits that hold their shape, wash after wash, season after season.

  • ✓ Every design hand-digitized — no auto-trace shortcuts
  • ✓ Embroidered, PVC, woven & chenille patch options
  • ✓ Full team kit production, from jerseys to numbering
  • ✓ Free samples before your full production run
