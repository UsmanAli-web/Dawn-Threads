How We Digitize

Every design that comes through the shop is mapped out by someone who's actually worked an embroidery machine — not run through auto-digitizing software. We plan stitch direction, density, and underlay based on the fabric it's going on, so the file runs clean the first time.

Left Chest & Cap Files

Small-format logos need tight, balanced stitching to stay sharp at a compact size. We build left chest and cap files specifically for their placement, so nothing distorts or drifts on curved or narrow surfaces.

Jacket Backs & Large Formats

Big designs need careful splitting and alignment to avoid puckering on thick fabric. We plan large-format files section by section so they stitch out smooth across the full design.

Puff & Specialty Digitizing

3D puff, towel, and other specialty digitizing each need their own density and underlay settings. We build files for the specific technique and fabric, not a one-size-fits-all template.

What's Included
